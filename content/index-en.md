# Antonio Castaldi

My name is Antonio Castaldi but everyone calls me *Toto*, feel free to do the same :)

Here are some parts of me:

## Entrepreneurship

First as a freelancer, then in a general partnership, and finally for nearly twenty years as founder and manager of [Skillbill srl](https://www.skillbill.it/).

I believe in co-creating work environments where people want to be. Collaboration, transparency, and care are my core values.

## Fitness

I have been training consistently and with dedication for many years. It makes me feel good and is one of the reasons I wake up every day.

I work as a [Personal Trainer and Pilates instructor](/en/qualifications) at two gyms in the Milan metropolitan area.

## Making

I enjoy "tinkering" with electronics and computer science, as and inventor. You can find some stuff out there, but the one I’m currently most active with is [ArturoBot](https://www.arturobot.it/).

## Computer Science

I enjoy staying up to date on algorithms, languages, architectures, and solutions. I do it both out of passion and for work.

My [GitHub repositories](https://github.com/toto-castaldi) and some [projects I have created](https://toto-castaldi.com).

## Nonviolent Communication

Marshall Rosenberg's Nonviolent Communication places emotions and needs at the centre. I have studied it extensively and find it an exceptional tool for connecting with myself and others. I use it daily.
